or legend, on every form. Height is reserved (`.errorslot` has a min-height) so an appearing error does not shift the form under a thumb already on its way down. `role="alert"` announces it on a full page post, which is why `autofocus` is NOT used here — moving focus and announcing would fight each other. It is the only assertive region in the product; the flash (§4.8) is polite. The summary links to each failing field by id, and the field-level message renders beside the field as well (`_field.html`). The typed value is never cleared. {% endcomment %}
