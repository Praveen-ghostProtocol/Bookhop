We aim to reply the same day — we're a small team."* On a page where **nothing stores what a student writes**. A student would type a problem, be told it was read and answered today, and the message would go nowhere. Worse than silence: *a student who knows there is no channel uses one that exists; a student who believes a message was read waits.* `help.scope` read: *"We can help you reach the other student, put a book back up, or look at a listing. The relay is **S5** (A-6), listings are **S2**, and there is no channel to ask through in any case. **Only the fact survives**, which is also the half that carries weight: it is the vouching liability COMPLIANCE §2.5 and R-16 call our worst asymmetry, and `tests/e2e/test_first_run_journey.py::test_the_help_page_says_what_bookhop_cannot_do_before_anything_else` asserts exactly that half and not the promises — QA had already drawn the line in the right place. Both are **predictions about our own future conduct**, which COPY-DECK §1.2 forbids and which no grep can catch: neither contains a banned word. Code review read for it, which is the division of labour THREAT-MODEL §6.1 states outright — the grep catches words, the reviewer catches sentences, and neither substitutes for the other. `help.no_channel` replaces `help.response` and names the state instead: there is no way to send BookHop a message from inside the app yet. The grievance-officer link below is the one statutory route, and that page states its own condition rather than this one making a claim on its behalf. **Why the page still ships.** Everything left on it is a fact about the product or an instruction, and the trust answers are the copy the product most needs a student to read before they ever meet a listing.
