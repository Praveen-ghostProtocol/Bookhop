THERE IS NO TICKET FORM, AND THAT IS NOT AN OMISSION. J9 is marked ⛔ BLOCKED (CG-2) in JOURNEYS Appendix B: every support L1 function takes `staff`, there is no endpoint, and nothing stores what a student writes. A form that posted nowhere would be worse than no form, and adding a service function is a contract change that is not mine to make. What ships is the part that is buildable and honest. WHAT WE CAN AND CANNOT DO IS STATED **BEFORE** THE STUDENT READS ANYTHING ELSE (AC-H6.5). Saying it up front is cheaper than saying it in a reply, and it is the one unmanaged expectation that turns into the belief that BookHop vouches for the trade. "We can't refund you — we never hold the money" is ADR-003 said out loud. NO PHONE NUMBER AND NO WHATSAPP NUMBER appears here or anywhere in the product, the emails or the notices (AC-H6.4, PRD EX-7). An unlogged channel makes support minutes unmeasurable, and the support number is what D-009's reversal trigger reads. No SLA number for help (J9 step 7). Statutory clocks apply to reports, grievances and disputes and those are stated exactly where they apply; promising a time one staff member cannot keep is how a help surface becomes a complaint surface.
