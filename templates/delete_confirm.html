"Nothing else is kept" would have been the same defect mirrored — equally unfalsifiable from the screen, and wrong the moment a ninth retention lands. What the card asserts is only what it enumerates. The email-hash item leads the new group deliberately: it is the one a reasonable person accepts when told and resents when discovered, so it names the mechanism (a code made from the address), the period (one year) and the purpose (a banned account cannot sign up again). It does not reassure — no "your data is safe", no "don't worry". Describing a mechanism and naming a purpose is exactly what COPY-DECK §1.2 permits; a prediction about the outcome would not be. `repeat_infringer_records` is **deliberately not in this list**, and that is recorded rather than overlooked.
