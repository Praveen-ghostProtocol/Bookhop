TWO THINGS HERE ARE REQUIRED BY NAME AND THEY PULL AGAINST EACH OTHER. (1) THE ADDRESS IS ECHOED. This is the student's own submitted address, shown to themselves, and it is not a disclosure. (2) THE ACCEPTED DOMAINS ARE NAMED, GENERICALLY. AC-A1.2 calls this "a product requirement, not polish": without it, the enumeration-proof 202 and L1's `EmailDomainNotRecognised` combine into a silent dead end that the PRD names as the most likely first-run failure in the product. EC-11 covers the empty case — no MoU yet, so no domains — where the page says the campus is not open for sign-ups.
