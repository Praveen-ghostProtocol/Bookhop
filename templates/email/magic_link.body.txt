  The magic-link email body. `templates/email/magic_link.body.txt`, paired with
  `.subject.txt` — the two filenames `apps/core/mail.py` renders. Two files so
  the subject is translatable and reviewable as copy rather than being a Python
  literal with a %s in it.

  PLAIN TEXT ONLY. An HTML email buys nothing for one link and costs bytes on a
  metered connection — and a Gmail/Outlook HTML pipeline is one more place a
  link can be rewritten or prefetched.

  THE CONTEXT DICT IS BACKEND'S CONTRACT AND THIS TEMPLATE USES THREE OF ITS
  FIVE KEYS. `apps/accounts/jobs.py send_magic_link` passes:
      token, expires_at, expires_in_minutes, display_name, campus_name
  and its own comment says "the template builds the URL, because the route is
  web/urls.py's and frontend owns it" — so the path `/auth/c/{token}` is
  assembled here and matches `web/urls.py`.

  `display_name` and `campus_name` are deliberately NOT used. A sign-in email
  is the one message that may arrive at an address whose owner did not ask for
  it (someone typing a classmate's address by mistake, or on purpose). Greeting
  them by name, or naming their campus, would confirm to a stranger that an
  account exists and which campus it is on — the same account-enumeration leak
  the 202 on `POST /auth/start` exists to prevent, moved into the mailbox.
  Nothing here names the campus, the domain or any other account attribute.

  `scheme` and `host` are NOT in that context dict, which is a real gap: a job
  has no request to read an absolute URL from. Flagged to backend — a relative
  link is not clickable in a mail client, so one of `scheme`/`host` or a
  pre-built absolute URL has to be added to the payload. Until then these render
  empty and the link is broken, which is loud rather than silent.

  COPY-DECK §10.5 rule (b): **no notification or email ever contains a rupee
  amount.** There is none here and there is nothing to add one to.

  `expires_in_minutes` is interpolated rather than the literal "10": the TTL is
  `TOKEN_TTL` in the service layer, and a hard-coded 10 here would be a second
  copy of it free to diverge (CLAUDE.md rule 10). COPY-DECK's `auth.sent.body`
  says "10 minutes" as a literal on the link-sent page, which is the same
  divergence one step removed — flagged to design-director.
{% endcomment %}
